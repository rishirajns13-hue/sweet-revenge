# Ideas to talk about together

_Started 13 Nov 2026. Nothing here is built or promised; we pick together, and new games are only built after we've discussed them._

## What's already in (so we don't suggest it twice)
- **Chat:** messages, stickers, an emoji wheel, voice notes, "is typing…", a 👉 Poke that shakes your friend's screen, and hover times on messages.
- **Home screen:** a revenge call-out after a friend beats you; levels, trophies and rivals; your own critter and theme.

## Chat: ways to make it better
1. **React to a message:** tap a message to stick an emoji on it (😂 on their trash talk). Small, and very "us".
2. **Reply to a message:** quote what you're answering ("↪ 'you'll never catch me'"). Mostly useful in Doodle Duel and longer Ludo games.
3. **Chat that carries over:** typed messages are already saved. When you rejoin a room (or the next game with the same friend), show the last few lines so the banter continues.
4. **Taunt packs per friend:** let each of us save 3 personal one-tap lines ("Kunju-special: 'skill issue'"), shown in the quick row.
5. **Voice note reactions:** play a voice note and the sender's critter "talks" (mouth moving) on the board.
6. **End-of-game chat moment:** after a game, a 10-second "say something" window with the winner's critter flexing and the loser's sulking, with big buttons for GG / rematch / "I want a recount".

## Other ways to interact (outside a game)
1. **Challenge a friend:** a button on Rivals that sends "Kunju challenges you to Plus Four 🃏". It shows on their home screen (and as a toast if they're online), and accepting creates the room for both.
2. **Who's online:** a little green dot on a friend's critter when they have the site open (Supabase Realtime presence). Tap it to challenge them.
3. **Bets:** before a game, each of you picks a silly stake ("loser sends a voice note singing"), which is shown on the end screen.
4. **Grudge match tracker:** a running series ("Ludo: best of 5, Kunju leads 2–1") that keeps going across days, with a trophy for winning the series.
5. **Daily "revenge of the day":** one small daily challenge for both of you (win a Plus Four with a stacked +4) that gives bonus XP.
6. **Shared photo wall:** saved Doodle Duel drawings and funny end screens on a "Hall of Shame" tab.
7. **Birthday / anniversary mode:** on a date we set, the whole site gets a special theme and confetti on login.

## Game ideas (for discussion: meaningful ones only, no throwaway minigames)
Each one plays well with two people, has real decisions, and creates "revenge" moments.
1. **Secret Signal (co-op word spy):** one gives a one-word clue and the other must find the right words on a 5×5 grid while avoiding the bomb. Played as a team against the clock.
2. **How Well Do You Know Me?:** each answers questions about themselves, the other guesses. Built for a pair of close friends, and great with voice notes.
3. **Carrom (flick game):** an original take on board flicking: aim and power with the mouse or arrows, pocket your colour, "queen" bonus. Turn-based, so it works over the relay.
4. **Battleship-style "Bonk Fleet":** hidden boards, take turns guessing, with special shots earned by revenge hits.
5. **Word race (Wordle-style, head to head):** same secret word, two boards; you see the other's colours live but not their letters.
6. **Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe:** nine boards in one; quick to learn and deeply tactical.
7. **Card Clash (original deck-builder lite):** small decks of silly cards (Bonk, Steal, Shield), 10-minute duels.
8. **Spot the Sneaky Change:** one changes something in a scene, the other finds it, then swap.

## More fun, more animated, better looking
1. **Critters live everywhere:** your critter sits beside your piece in Ludo, cheers when you bonk, sulks when bonked (the moods already exist).
2. **Victory screens with personality:** the winner's critter does a dance, the loser's gets a little rain cloud; a "revenge owed" stamp goes on the loser's profile.
3. **Seasonal themes:** Diwali lights, monsoon rain, Christmas snow, switched on automatically for a few days.
4. **Sound pass:** a few more real recorded sounds (dice rattle, card slap, crowd "ooh" on a bonk), all quiet and optional.
5. **Level-up moment:** a short full-screen cone that grows a scoop, with each friend's critter clapping if they're online.
6. **Trophy room:** a 3D-ish shelf where trophies wobble when you hover; rare trophies sparkle.
7. **Haptics on phones:** a tiny buzz on bonks, bombs and pokes.
8. **Home screen "story":** a short line of what happened since you last looked ("Ammu levelled up to Sundae and unlocked Bomb Squad 🧨").

## Small fixes noticed while testing (13 Nov)
- In practice games the chat says "Say hi", but it's just you and bots. Idea: bots post a taunt now and then (they already throw emoji in Plus Four and Snakes).
- The Ludo page's "No games yet" card is plain; it could show your last game against a friend instead.
- If someone navigates very fast between pages, the browser sometimes abandons the page fade. It's harmless and not visible.
